Shiatsu

Shiatsu is a Japanese bodywork therapy that uses finger and palm pressure, stretches and joint manipulations to support the body's natural healing processes. Rooted in Traditional Chinese Medicine, it works with the flow of Ki (vital energy) through the body's meridian pathways.

Whether you're looking to reduce stress, manage chronic pain, or simply achieve a deeper sense of wellbeing, Shiatsu offers a holistic approach that addresses the whole person — body, mind and spirit.
