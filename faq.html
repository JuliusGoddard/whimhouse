The fridge should already be on when you arrive. If not, pull it towards you and switch it on at the wall. The other wall switch behind the fridge powers the cooking element. Again, if it is not already switched on please do so. There is a switch to the left of the bathroom door for the hot water and another above the bathroom door for the shower and the bathroom towel rail.

There are two heaters in the pod. 1 A tubular trickle-charger which stops the pod from getting too cold when not occupied – especially in winter. Please feel free to leave this on all day and all night if you so wish. If you don’t want to use it and you find it getting in the way, you can just put it behind the sofa. 2 A wall panel heater for heating the room up very quickly.

The pods are self-catering. We provide a welcome pack of tea, coffee, milk and biscuits but recommend you bring some food with you – especially for breakfast on the first morning. Your pod is fully equipped with crockery, cutlery and cooking utensils. There is also a microwave and two burners. Please note these burners can get very hot indeed so please take care not to touch them during use and not to place anything on them but cooking pots and pans. There is no local shop within walking distance. There is, however, a very convenient farm shop a 5 minute drive to the south on the A701 called Whitmuir. It also has an excellent restaurant offering both breakfasts and lunch. Opening hours: Monday to Friday 9.30am-4pm and Saturday to Sunday 9am-4pm Whitmuir Farm Shop 10 minutes to the north in Penicuik there is a full range of food shops and supermarkets including Farm Foods, Lidl and Tesco. There are also several pubs, cafes and takeaways. Menus for the takeaways are in the pod. If you decide to order from one of these and they’re not sure of the location, tell them we at the rear of Whim Hall Care Home.

All pods are equipped with smoke detectors and first-aid kits. Walking paths are illuminated with soft, low-impact evening lighting to maintain dark-sky compliance while ensuring safe navigation.
